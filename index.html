Our Approach

Mind, body, spirit, and gut health — treated as one connected system, not four separate appointments.

Essential Wellness Loft is a women-owned wellness studio in Tracy built around a holistic view of health. Clients work with the same small team of practitioners across modalities — massage, essential oil applications, energy work, and bioenergetic scanning — so care stays personal and continuous, rather than starting over at every visit.

Every treatment plan is tailored to the individual. Practitioners take the time to listen before recommending anything, and the studio itself is designed to make that slowing-down easy — soft lighting, quiet rooms, and a lounge meant for sitting a while before or after your session.

What We Offer

A holistic approach, one session at a time.

Every service is offered one-on-one and shaped around what your body needs that day — book a single session or build a plan across modalities.

I

Massage Therapy

Hands-on bodywork to ease tension, improve circulation, and help you reset — tailored to what your body's carrying that week.

II

Aroma Touch

A relaxing, guided application of essential oils along the back and feet, designed to calm the nervous system and restore balance.

III

Bioenergetic Testing

Non-invasive scanning used to get a fuller picture of what's going on in the body, and to guide a personalized wellness plan.

IV

Energy Work

Gentle, hands-on sessions aimed at releasing blocked energy and supporting the body's own capacity to heal and recover.

The Studio

A room built for slowing down.

Warm neutrals, low light, and quiet corners — the loft is designed to feel less like a waiting room and more like an exhale.
